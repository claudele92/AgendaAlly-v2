description: The creator's visual direction and limits for AgendaAlly's seeded Service catalog.
---

AgendaAlly is an Africa-first marketplace. People-centered demo Service
photography should predominantly and naturally represent Black/African
customers and professionals, while keeping relevant natural diversity.
Service accuracy comes first; appropriate treatment details, tools or finished
work are preferable to an unrelated photograph chosen for representation.

**Why:** The creator stated that the demo catalog should reflect its initial
market rather than look like a generic European/North-American stock catalog.

**How to apply:** Curate demo content only. Do not add race/ethnicity fields
or demographic behavior to accounts, Services, Shops, search, ranking, booking
or permissions. Do not infer nationality or ethnicity from appearance; use
source descriptions with explicit editorial qualifications when reporting
representation counts. Preserve legitimate Vendor media and native ownership.
