PHASE 2 — IMPROVE THE EXISTING TERMS OF SERVICE
==================================================

Improve the current Terms so they more completely and professionally describe AgendaAlly's actual marketplace and service-booking platform.

Preserve useful existing sections such as account responsibilities, acceptable use, intellectual property, limitations, termination, dispute language or other general provisions where they remain applicable and accurate.

Improve or add platform-specific language where needed for the implemented model.

The Terms should accurately describe the relationships among:

- AgendaAlly
- Customers
- Vendors/Shops
- Specialists/staff

Do not characterize AgendaAlly as providing capabilities or financial services that the application does not provide.

The Terms must respect these established platform boundaries:

1. Booking state and financial state are separate.

2. CANCELLED does not mean REFUNDED.

3. REFUND REQUESTED does not mean refunded.

4. REFUND APPROVED does not mean money has moved.

5. PAYOUT APPROVED does not mean the Vendor has been paid.

6. Only authoritative financial completion may be represented as a completed refund or payout.

7. REQUIRES_REVIEW represents an unresolved financial operation requiring review/reconciliation and must not be described as completed.

8. PAYMENT SELECTED does not necessarily mean PAYMENT COLLECTED.

9. Cash being selected does not prove that Cash was actually collected.

10. Wallet-funded transactions retain their supported internal Wallet semantics.

11. AgendaAlly does not provide Wallet-to-cash redemption unless that capability is separately implemented and authorized in the future.

12. Do not promise automatic/provider-driven refunds or payouts.

13. Do not imply arbitrary partial refunds are supported.

14. Platform financial obligations must not be expanded beyond the implemented and accepted model.

15. Platform payout workflows concern eligible Vendors where applicable.

16. Specialist compensation is the responsibility of the Vendor/Shop.

Specialist compensation is Shop-funded and Shop-controlled under the relationship between the Vendor/Shop and Specialist.

AgendaAlly must NOT be described as the payer of Specialist compensation.

Do not create Specialist platform payout rights or obligations.

17. Vendor-direct payment scenarios must only be described to the extent actually implemented/available. Do not imply AgendaAlly takes custody of funds that it does not collect.

18. AgendaAlly's role as marketplace/booking platform should be distinguished from the responsibilities of independent Vendors/Shops for the services they provide.

Do not invent broader liability allocations beyond what the existing Terms already establish without flagging them for owner/legal review.

The Terms should reference the separate Refund & Cancellation Policy for detailed cancellation/refund rules instead of duplicating the entire policy.

PHASE 4 — ADD A SEPARATE REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY
==================================================

Create a distinct first-class Refund & Cancellation Policy using the existing AgendaAlly policy/content architecture where practical.

This should be a Customer-readable policy, not a technical description of the state machine.

It must nevertheless accurately reflect the implemented semantics.

The policy must distinguish:

BOOKING CANCELLATION
→ REFUND ELIGIBILITY
→ REFUND REQUEST
→ REVIEW / APPROVAL
→ FINANCIAL COMPLETION

These are not the same event.

The policy should explain, using clear Customer-facing language:

- when a booking may be cancelled, based only on authoritative existing rules
- how cancellation eligibility is determined
- cancellation fees where authoritative policy defines them
- refund eligibility
- how the eligible refund amount is determined
- that the server/platform-authoritative amount governs the request
- refund requests
- review/approval
- completion
- REQUIRES_REVIEW or equivalent unresolved review situations
- Cash transactions
- legitimately funded Wallet transactions
- platform-collected transactions where supported
- Vendor-direct transactions only where actually supported
- duplicate/replayed requests not creating additional refund entitlement
- circumstances in which no refund is due ONLY where those circumstances already exist in authoritative business rules

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT turn test/acceptance fixture values into global legal rules.

For example, a 50% cancellation-fee value used in a financial acceptance fixture must NOT become a universal AgendaAlly cancellation policy unless an authoritative production business rule explicitly defines it that way.

Do not invent:

- refund windows
- cancellation windows
- percentages
- fixed fees
- processing-day promises
- refund completion timelines
- cancellation penalties
- refund exceptions
- provider-specific promises

when those rules are not already authoritative.

Where cancellation/refund rules are configurable by Shop/Vendor or otherwise dynamic, describe that model accurately rather than hard-coding an example value into the policy.

Do not claim that AgendaAlly automatically executes external provider refunds.

The accepted MVP financial model uses controlled/manual financial execution where applicable.

Approval must not be described as completion.
