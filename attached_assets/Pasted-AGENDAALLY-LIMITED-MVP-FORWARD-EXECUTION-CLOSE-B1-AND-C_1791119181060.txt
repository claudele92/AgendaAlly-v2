3. CLOSED BUSINESS / SCHEDULING DECISIONS — DO NOT ASK AGAIN
======================================================================

The following decisions are CONFIRMED and CLOSED.

A. CAPACITY OWNER

B. INTERVAL RULE

Use half-open intervals:

    [start, end)

Therefore:

    existing 09:00–10:00
    requested 10:00–11:00

is allowed.

But:

    existing 09:00–10:00
    requested 09:30–10:30

must be rejected.

Bookings may touch exactly at their stored endpoints.


C. PROCESSING / PAUSE TIME

Stored end already includes:

    service duration + processing/pause time

exactly once.

Do NOT add another artificial buffer.

Adjacency is evaluated against the stored end.


D. WORKING HOURS

The complete stored appointment interval must fit within the specialist's
working interval.

If working hours end at 18:00:

    17:00–18:00

may be valid if otherwise available.

    17:30–18:30

must be rejected.


E. MONTHLY RECURRENCE

Monthly recurrence preserves the ORIGINAL CALENDAR-DAY ANCHOR.

Example:

    original = January 31

Occurrences are:

    Jan 31
    Feb = skipped if Feb 31 does not exist
    Mar 31
    Apr = skipped if Apr 31 does not exist
    May 31
    ...

Do NOT clamp to the final day of the month.

Do NOT drift the recurrence anchor after a skipped month.


F. COUNTED RECURRENCE

"End after N occurrences" counts scheduled recurrence occurrences beginning
with the original rule occurrence.

The first/original occurrence counts as occurrence 1.

Occurrence count is based on recurrence generation, NOT whether that occurrence
falls on:
- a working day;
- an open Shop day;
- a Customer-visible availability day;
- a later queried availability range.

Closed dates and working hours do not reset or extend the recurrence count.

Example:

    start = Jan 1
    daily
    end after 3

means:

    Jan 1 = #1
    Jan 2 = #2
    Jan 3 = #3

The recurrence is finished after Jan 3 even if Jan 2 was otherwise closed.


G. RECURRENCE ORIGIN

Recurring disabled-time evaluation must be anchored to the original recurrence
rule.

It must NOT depend on the requested availability window containing the original
rule date.

A daily recurring block beginning Jan 7 must still be evaluated when Jan 14 is
requested.


H. CLEANUP

Scheduled cleanup must not guess recurrence semantics.

For counted recurring rules, cleanup may remove/archive the rule only when the
true final recurrence is definitively in the past under the confirmed recurrence
contract.

Do not delete a counted rule merely because:
- its original date is old;
- today's date is outside the current query range;
- a working day was closed;
- an occurrence was not bookable.

Do not remove a recurrence on its inclusive final occurrence before that
occurrence has passed.

If safe automatic cleanup cannot be proven from the stored rule, retaining the
rule is preferable to premature deletion.


I. TIMEZONE

The existing backend comparison baseline is UTC and current persisted/request
times are naive date-time strings.

Do NOT invent a new timezone conversion architecture in this phase.

Preserve existing clock behavior.

However, explicitly test and document:
- midnight boundaries;
- current UTC behavior;
- Customer/Vendor rendering consistency;
- whether existing UI/API transformations shift the selected booking time.

A material Shop-timezone/Customer-timezone redesign is a STOP boundary.
