## TARGET PAYMENT MODEL

The future architecture must support TWO vendor payment modes.

### Vendor Direct

Vendor configures and uses its own supported payment gateway.

### Platform Collection

Vendor enables:

"Let AgendaAlly collect customer payments"

AgendaAlly/platform gateway collects customer payment.

Then:

Customer payment
→ AgendaAlly
→ commission calculation
→ vendor net
→ vendor ledger
→ payout

Admin must ultimately be able to:

- allow vendor choice
- force Vendor Direct
- force Platform Collection

Payment mode must apply to both service bookings and product orders.
