AgendaAlly — Implement Approved Shop Profile Redesign + Service Images

Implement the approved Customer storefront Shop/Business Profile redesign.

The supplied/approved mockup is the visual direction for this task.

This is an implementation pass, not another design-proposal pass.

The key design decision is:

ONE BUSINESS PROFILE, MULTIPLE OFFERINGS.

A business may offer:
- Services only;
- Products only;
- both Services and Products.

Services and Products must NOT compete as narrow side-by-side catalog columns.

The redesigned Shop profile should instead provide a coherent marketplace experience where Services, Products and Specialists are first-class sections of the same business profile.

Service imagery is also approved and should be implemented as part of this redesign.

IMPORTANT:
- preserve existing booking behavior;
- preserve existing product/cart behavior;
- preserve public/private Specialist behavior;
- do not merge Service booking and Product checkout;
- do not modify payment architecture;
- do not proceed into unrelated work.

Credit use is important. Audit only what is necessary to implement this correctly.
