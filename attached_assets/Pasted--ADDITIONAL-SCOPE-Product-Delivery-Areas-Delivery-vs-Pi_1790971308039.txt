# 19. Fix incorrect mandatory Area behavior where appropriate

The Product checkout must not require delivery-specific geography when it is irrelevant.

The expected rule should conceptually be:

DELIVERY
→ customer delivery address/location information may be required according to the existing delivery model.

PICKUP
→ delivery Area should NOT be required merely because delivery checkout requires it.

Do not mechanically remove Area validation globally.

Determine the correct validation boundary.

If Area is genuinely necessary for delivery pricing/eligibility:

- keep it mandatory for applicable Delivery orders;
- make the requirement server-authoritative;
- ensure the selected Area belongs to the selected City/Country;
- ensure the Vendor actually serves that Area if Vendor delivery restrictions exist.

For Pickup:

- do not require a customer delivery Area;
- do not calculate a delivery fee;
- preserve the shop/location selected for pickup.

If the current backend requires Area even for Pickup, fix that condition cleanly.
