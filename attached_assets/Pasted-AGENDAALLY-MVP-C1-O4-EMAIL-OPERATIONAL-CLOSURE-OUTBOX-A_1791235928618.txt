9. SUBSCRIPTION RECIPIENT PRIVACY FIX
==================================================

The audit confirmed that subscription mail can place all subscribers in visible To headers.

Fix this before subscription delivery can be considered safe.

Preferred architecture:

ONE LOGICAL DELIVERY PER RECIPIENT.

Each subscriber should receive a message addressed only to that subscriber.

Do not expose another subscriber's email address in:

- To;
- CC;
- body;
- unsubscribe/action link;
- logs;
- error responses.

Do not merely move every subscriber into one giant BCC message if the existing architecture can reasonably support isolated per-recipient delivery.

Per-recipient delivery is preferred because it provides better:

- privacy;
- failure isolation;
- unsubscribe semantics;
- auditing;
- future personalization.

Preserve the existing subscription business semantics.

Do NOT activate subscription delivery or scheduler during this campaign unless later explicitly approved for acceptance.
