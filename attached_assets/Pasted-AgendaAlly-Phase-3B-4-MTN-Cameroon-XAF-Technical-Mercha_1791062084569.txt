4. AGENDAALLY BUSINESS DESCRIPTION
============================================================

Prepare a short neutral description of AgendaAlly for MTN.

Describe it as a multi-Vendor marketplace/service-booking platform where:

- Customers purchase Products and book Services from independent Vendors;
- AgendaAlly may collect electronic payment through a platform-managed payment
  method;
- AgendaAlly calculates a platform commission;
- the remaining Vendor entitlement is recorded internally;
- Vendor settlement/payout is handled separately from Customer collection;
- Cash, Wallet and electronic payment methods have separate custody semantics.

Do not claim that AgendaAlly is:

- already an MTN merchant;
- already approved as an aggregator;
- a licensed payment institution;
- an MTN partner;
- an MTN payment facilitator;
- certified for Cameroon/XAF.

The purpose of the inquiry is partly to determine what commercial/onboarding
classification MTN requires.

8. ASYNCHRONOUS IDENTITY / RETRY CONTRACT
============================================================

Explain only enough of AgendaAlly's design for MTN to validate it:

AgendaAlly creates and durably commits a unique server-owned MTN request
reference before RequestToPay.

If the RequestToPay result is ambiguous, AgendaAlly retains that reference and
queries the original transaction rather than generating another reference.

Ask MTN to confirm:

A. Is `X-Reference-Id` the correct durable identity for subsequent
RequestToPay status lookup?

B. After an ambiguous timeout, should the merchant query that reference rather
than submit a new RequestToPay?

C. What happens if RequestToPay is submitted again with the same
`X-Reference-Id`?

D. Is reuse rejected, idempotent, or otherwise defined?

E. What retry/reconciliation procedure does MTN recommend after an uncertain
network result?

Do not ask MTN to review AgendaAlly source code.
