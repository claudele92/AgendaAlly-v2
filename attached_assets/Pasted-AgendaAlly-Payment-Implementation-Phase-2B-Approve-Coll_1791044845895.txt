AgendaAlly — Payment Implementation Phase 2B
Approve Collection/Allocation Schema Migration and Bounded Implementation

The finalized Phase 2 collection/allocation design in:

docs/development/payment-phase2-collection-allocation.md

is approved as the implementation baseline.

I now approve implementation of the finalized two-layer financial architecture
and its required migrations, subject to the boundaries below.

This approval does NOT authorize provider activation, Vendor-direct checkout
enablement, provider refunds, external payout rails, legacy financial
classification, or publishing.

============================================================
1. APPROVED ARCHITECTURE
============================================================

Implement the finalized design consisting conceptually of:

1. SHOP ECONOMIC PAYMENT ALLOCATION

A durable, once-only Shop-level economic accounting identity representing:

- gross economic amount;
- AgendaAlly commission calculated exactly once;
- commission satisfied from platform-controlled funds;
- outstanding Vendor commission receivable;
- Vendor economic entitlement;
- platform-held Vendor payable;
- refunds/reversals;
- settlement effects;
- remaining liability;
- accounting/finality state.

2. IMMUTABLE FUNDING / COLLECTION CONTRIBUTIONS

Contribution-level immutable evidence representing how portions of the
economic allocation were actually funded/collected, including:

- collection mode;
- collector/custody ownership;
- provider where applicable;
- amount;
- currency;
- safe merchant/config ownership identity where required;
- original funding evidence/identity;
- contribution finality.

Do not store provider credentials, API secrets, tokens or other secrets.

Use the exact finalized columns, constraints, keys, states and lifecycle from
the approved Phase 2 design unless implementation discovers a correctness
problem.

============================================================
2. ACCOUNTING POLICY
============================================================

Preserve the finalized accounting policy.

Commission is calculated ONCE per Shop economic allocation.

Do not calculate full commission independently for:

- each Transaction;
- each callback;
- each funding contribution;
- replacement Transactions.

Platform-controlled funds satisfy AgendaAlly commission first.

Remaining platform-controlled funds may become Vendor payable.

Vendor-direct and Vendor-received Cash principal must NOT create a platform
payout liability for principal already received directly by the Vendor.

If platform-controlled funds are insufficient to satisfy commission, the
remaining commission becomes Vendor commission receivable.

Never create a negative Vendor payable.

============================================================
3. WALLET CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

Preserve the finalized Wallet conclusion:

A verified Customer Wallet debit represents PLATFORM-CONTROLLED funding for
allocation purposes because the Customer's internal balance is reduced without
that value independently entering the Vendor's merchant account.

This does NOT mean Wallet is an external provider.

It is a custody/accounting classification.

Example that must remain correct:

Gross: 100
Commission: 10
Wallet: 40
Vendor-direct: 60

Result:

Commission calculated = 10
Commission satisfied = 10
Vendor payable from platform-held funds = 30
Vendor direct receipt = 60
Commission receivable = 0

Economic reconciliation:

30 + 60 + 10 = 100

The Vendor-direct 60 must never also become platform payable.

============================================================
4. CASH CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

Where native Cash semantics establish that the Vendor receives Customer Cash
directly:

- Cash principal is Vendor-controlled/directly received;
- it does not create duplicate platform payable;
- AgendaAlly commission may become commission receivable according to the
  approved allocation model.

Do not fabricate platform custody of Cash.

Preserve the already verified Cash authorization/payment boundaries.

============================================================
5. PLATFORM-MANAGED ELECTRONIC
============================================================

For platform-managed electronic collection:

- platform merchant credentials collect/hold the Customer contribution for
  accounting purposes;
- contribution is classified as platform-controlled;
- AgendaAlly commission is satisfied from platform-controlled value according
  to the approved policy;
- remaining Vendor entitlement may become platform Vendor payable.

Do NOT activate a provider to test this.

Use synthetic verified-provider fixtures through existing trusted verification
paths.

============================================================
6. VENDOR-DIRECT
============================================================

For Vendor-direct electronic collection:

- the contribution belongs to the Vendor merchant relationship;
- Vendor-direct principal is NOT platform-held;
- that principal must NOT create platform Vendor payout liability;
- AgendaAlly still records the economic transaction;
- commission is calculated once at the Shop allocation;
- commission may be satisfied from other platform-held contributions;
- otherwise the unsatisfied portion remains Vendor commission receivable.

Do NOT yet enable `Use my own payment gateway`.

This phase builds the accounting architecture required for it.

============================================================
7. ORIGINAL CONTRIBUTION EVIDENCE
============================================================

Funding/collection contributions must remain immutable evidence of the original
custody split.

Later changes to:

- Shop payment preference;
- provider configuration;
- provider activation;
- environment flags;
- country/currency policy;
- Vendor merchant settings

must not reinterpret an existing financial allocation.

Refunds, payouts and reconciliation must eventually be able to operate from
this persisted original context rather than current settings.

============================================================
8. IDEMPOTENCY / ECONOMIC IDENTITY
============================================================

Implement the finalized economic-allocation identity and uniqueness rules.

A replacement Transaction must NOT create:

- another Shop economic allocation;
- another full commission;
- another Vendor entitlement;
- another Vendor payable.

Duplicate callback must not duplicate allocation.

Duplicate paid transition must not duplicate allocation.

Shared funding receipt must not duplicate allocation.

Stale model must not duplicate allocation.

Use the finalized allocation/contribution/effect keys from the approved design.

Do not fall back to Transaction ID as the sole economic identity if the design
explicitly separates those concepts.

============================================================
9. PLATFORM FEE LEDGER
============================================================

Integrate `platform_fee_ledger_entries` according to the finalized design.

Its economic accounting must ultimately be tied to the durable economic
allocation rather than allowing replacement Transactions to create another
full economic fee/payable event.

Preserve historical records.

Do NOT rewrite historical fee-ledger entries merely to populate the new model.

Where the finalized design uses new allocation/effect references, implement
them exactly as approved.

============================================================
10. REFUND / SETTLEMENT EFFECT IDENTITIES
============================================================

Implement the finalized durable effect identities required to make later:

- refunds;
- reversals;
- Vendor settlement;
- commission reversal

idempotent against the economic allocation.

Do NOT implement missing provider refunds.

Do NOT implement external payout rails.

The purpose here is to establish the durable accounting identity and state
required by those future operations.

Original contribution evidence must remain immutable.

Effects must be represented separately rather than rewriting historical
custody evidence.

============================================================
11. INTEGER MONEY
============================================================

Use the finalized integer monetary-unit representation and frozen monetary
scale from the approved design.

Do not introduce floating-point financial calculations.

Verify:

- gross;
- commission;
- satisfied commission;
- commission receivable;
- Vendor entitlement;
- Vendor payable;
- refunds;
- settlement effects;
- remaining liability

reconcile exactly in integer units.

============================================================
12. PRODUCT INTEGRATION
============================================================

Integrate Product payment/accounting into the new model at the authoritative
trusted lifecycle point established by the design.

Do not derive historical financial meaning from current Shop settings.

For NEW eligible Product payments, create/use the appropriate:

Shop economic allocation
+
funding contribution(s)

according to actual verified collection context.

Multi-Shop checkout must create Shop-isolated economic allocations.

Shop A's collection context must not affect Shop B.

Vendor A's merchant ownership must never classify Vendor B's contribution.

============================================================
13. BOOKING INTEGRATION
============================================================

Integrate Booking payment/accounting into the same canonical model.

Do not remove useful existing Booking routing information merely because the
new model exists.

Instead ensure the new immutable financial context becomes authoritative for
accounting once created.

Booking lifecycle must remain separate from electronic payment proof.

Preserve Phase 1 `BookingPaymentAuthority` verification.

============================================================
14. LEGACY ORDERS — DO NOT DELETE OR CLASSIFY
============================================================

The existing 12 Product Orders currently have:

KEEP THEM.

Do NOT delete them.

Do NOT change them to `pending`.

Do NOT change them to `settled`.

Do NOT infer historical financial settlement.

Do NOT classify them as platform-managed or Vendor-direct from current mutable
state.

guesswork.

`unverified` is an intentional fail-closed legacy state.

These Orders preserve historical commerce/audit information while preventing
uncertain historical financial state from being executed again.

Legacy reconciliation is a separate future phase.
