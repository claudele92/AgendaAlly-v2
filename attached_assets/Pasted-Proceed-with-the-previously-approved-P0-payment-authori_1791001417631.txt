============================================================
SCOPE
============================================================

Fix ONLY the confirmed P0 cross-Shop payment authorization vulnerability
identified in:

docs/development/payment-system-full-audit.md

Confirmed vulnerable endpoint:

PUT /api/v1/payments/{type}/{id}/transactions

The audit found that an authenticated Seller can reach the Cash transaction
status mutation and attempt to mark another Shop's transaction:

- paid
- canceled

without first proving that the underlying payable belongs to a Shop that the
Seller is authorized to manage.

The audit stopped before exercising the vulnerability or changing financial
state.

============================================================
AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENT
============================================================

Before any Seller transaction-status mutation, the backend must
authoritatively establish BOTH:

1. the underlying payable belongs to a Shop that the authenticated Seller is
   authorized to manage;

AND

2. the Seller has the existing required payment-management
   permission/grant for that operation.

The global `seller` role alone must NOT be sufficient.

Resolve ownership from persisted authoritative relationships.

Do NOT trust request-provided:

- shop_id
- vendor_id
- seller_id
- owner_id
- order_id
- booking_id

as proof of ownership.

============================================================
PRODUCT TRANSACTIONS
============================================================

For Product transactions, resolve ownership through the canonical persisted:

Transaction
→ Order
→ Shop

relationship used by the native application.

A Seller authorized for Shop A may perform an otherwise-valid operation on
Shop A's transaction.

The same Seller must be denied for Shop B's transaction.

============================================================
SERVICE BOOKING TRANSACTIONS
============================================================

For Service transactions, resolve ownership through the canonical persisted:

Transaction
→ Booking
→ owning Shop/business

relationship already used by AgendaAlly.

Do not create a competing ownership model.

A Seller must not mutate another Shop's booking transaction.

============================================================
FAIL CLOSED
============================================================

If ownership cannot be authoritatively resolved because the payable is:

- missing;
- unsupported;
- ambiguous;
- inconsistent;
- deleted;
- or lacks a resolvable owning Shop;

deny Seller mutation.

Do not fall back to global Seller authority.

============================================================
PRESERVE ADMIN AUTHORITY
============================================================

Preserve legitimate existing Admin/Super Admin authority.

Do not accidentally require Admin users to have Seller Shop membership.

Explicitly distinguish:

ADMIN/SUPER ADMIN AUTHORITY

from

SHOP-SCOPED SELLER AUTHORITY.

Do not broaden Admin authority.

============================================================
STATUS TRANSITIONS
============================================================

Shop ownership alone must NOT grant arbitrary transaction-status authority.

Preserve the existing transaction state rules.

Determine whether an authorized same-Shop Seller is legitimately allowed to
set:

- paid;
- canceled;

for Cash transactions under the existing business contract.

Preserve legitimate same-Shop behavior.

Do not add new Seller status-transition permissions.

============================================================
DO NOT DISABLE CASH
============================================================

Do not contain this vulnerability by disabling Cash.

Cash exposed the missing authorization boundary; fix that boundary.

Do not change:

- Cash eligibility;
- Cash economics;
- Cash accounting;
- commissions;
- payouts;
- checkout availability.
