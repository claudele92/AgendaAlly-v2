The two-layer schema direction is approved conceptually, but the migration is NOT yet authorized.
Preserve the separation between:
1. Economic payment allocation — the durable, once-only Shop-level economic identity for gross amount, AgendaAlly commission, Vendor economic entitlement/liability and accounting finality.
2. Funding/collection context — immutable contribution-level evidence describing how portions of that economic allocation were funded/collected, including collection mode, collector ownership, provider where applicable, amount and currency, without storing credentials/secrets.
Before migration, revise the proposed schema against the following accounting policy:
Commission is calculated exactly once at the economic Shop-allocation level, not independently per Transaction or funding contribution.
Funding contributions determine custody and settlement consequences, not duplicate commission calculations.
For platform-managed electronic collection, AgendaAlly holds/controls the collected principal for accounting purposes; the applicable Vendor economic entitlement may therefore become a platform payable after subtracting the applicable commission/adjustments.
For Vendor-direct collection, the Vendor already collected that contribution directly. That principal must never become an AgendaAlly Vendor payout liability. AgendaAlly's commission obligation must instead be represented as satisfied commission or a Vendor commission receivable, depending on whether AgendaAlly actually holds sufficient funds from another contribution.
For Cash received directly by the Vendor, apply the same custody principle: do not create a platform payable for principal AgendaAlly never possessed. Commission may remain receivable.
For Wallet/platform-held value, determine from the already-traced Wallet architecture whether that contribution represents value controlled by AgendaAlly and therefore can fund Vendor payable/commission.
For mixed funding, use a platform-held-funds-first commission allocation rule:
- calculate total commission once at the economic allocation;
- apply available platform-held funds toward that commission first;
- remaining platform-held funds may become Vendor payable;
- Vendor-direct/Cash principal already received by the Vendor creates no duplicate platform payable;
- if platform-held funds are insufficient to satisfy commission, the unsatisfied remainder becomes Vendor commission receivable;
- never manufacture a negative Vendor payable;
- never duplicate gross, commission or Vendor entitlement across contributions.
